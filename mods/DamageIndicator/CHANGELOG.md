# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-30

- First release.
- Health bars over characters after they take damage, with a draining "recent damage" trail.
- Your own health bar (hidden at full health), movable and resizable with the mouse.
- Floating damage numbers (yours, others', knock-out and killing blows) and your damage/heal numbers.
- Yellow stun bar (tased, staggered, knocked down) and KO / DEAD badges.
- Native in-game settings screen (pause menu → Damage Indicator).
- Supports both IL2CPP (default) and Mono (`alternate`) branches.
