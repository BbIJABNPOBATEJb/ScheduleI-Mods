# Russian glossary (ru)

Consult this before translating a chunk; add new recurring terms as you go.

## Conventions

- UI instructions and system text address the player formally with "вы": "Нажмите", "Выберите", "Вы уверены?".
  Dialogue: NPCs say "ты" to the player unless they are formal (shopkeepers, police).
- Buttons and actions use the infinitive: "Купить", "Закрыть", "Применить настройки". ALL-CAPS keys stay ALL-CAPS.
- Input prompts with "(Hold)": "(Зажать) <действие>", e.g. "(Зажать) Употребить".
- "Click" → "Нажмите", "Click and hold" → "Нажмите и удерживайте"; the hold-key verb is "зажать".
- Proper names, brands, stores, places and strains stay in Latin: Benzies, Gas-Mart, Northtown, Downtown,
  Bleuball's Boutique, Bud's Bar, Albert Hoover, OG Kush, SpeedGrow, PGR.
- Ellipsis stays as three dots "..." (not "…"). Money stays "$" in front: "${0}".
