# Brazilian Portuguese glossary (pt-BR)

Consult this before translating a chunk. Use these terms exactly as written; if a term is missing, add it here
and keep using it.

## Conventions

- The player is always "você", never "tu" or "o senhor". UI instructions use the você-form imperative:
  "Clique", "Segure", "Selecione", "Escolha", "Aceite a oferta...", "Tem certeza?".
- Dialogue: NPCs also say "você" (casual Brazilian speech, with contractions like "tá", "pra", "cê" only when the
  character is very slangy). Street NPCs use slang freely ("mano", "parça", "cara", "grana", "bagulho", "os
  homi" for cops). Shopkeepers, police and officials stay polite ("o senhor" is NOT used; stay with "você").
  Keep the crude humor; don't soften swearing ("porra", "merda", "caralho" are fine where the English swears).
- Buttons, menu actions and input prompts use the infinitive: "Comprar", "Fechar", "Voltar", "Cancelar",
  "Aplicar configurações", "Adicionar ao carrinho". ALL-CAPS keys stay ALL-CAPS: "ACEITAR", "COMPRAR".
- Labels, titles and settings use sentence case: "Sensibilidade da câmera", "Pedidos ativos". Proper names,
  ranks and painting/song titles keep their capitals ("Dono do Pedaço", "Dia de Praia").
- "(Hold) X" → "(Segurar) X" with X in the infinitive: "(Segurar) Consumir", "(Segurar) Fumar", "(Segurar) Cheirar".
- "Click" → "Clique"; "Click and hold" → "Clique e segure"; "Press" → "Pressione"; "Hold" (in a sentence) → "segure".
  Right/left mouse button → "botão direito/esquerdo do mouse". Key names: SPACEBAR → ESPAÇO.
- Proper names, brands, stores, places and strains stay in Latin: Benzies, Gas-Mart, Northtown, Northville,
  Downtown, Bleuball's Boutique, Bud's Bar, Albert Hoover, OG Kush, SpeedGrow, PGR. Use articles naturally:
  "os Benzies", "no Gas-Mart".
- Ellipsis stays as three dots "..." (not "…"). Money stays "$" in front with no space: "${0}", "+${0} de bônus".
- Keep the English decimal/format of numbers as in the key; don't convert units.
