# French glossary (fr)

Consult this before translating a chunk and follow it strictly. Add new recurring terms as you go
(never change an existing entry without updating every chunk that uses it).

## Conventions

- **Address.** UI instructions, tooltips, system text and popups use **vous**: "Cliquez", "Appuyez sur",
  "Choisissez", "Êtes-vous sûr ?", "Voulez-vous vraiment... ?".
  **Dialogue:** NPCs **tutoient** the player (street slang, casual, cheeky). Formal NPCs (shopkeepers,
  real-estate agent, bank/hospital staff, police) **vouvoient**. The player's own dialogue choices
  tutoient street contacts and vouvoient formal NPCs.
- **Buttons, actions, interaction prompts, input prompts, quest objectives:** infinitive —
  "Acheter", "Annuler", "Fermer", "Ajouter au panier", "Sonner à l'interphone", "Récupérer la cache".
- **Capitalization:** sentence case, not English Title Case: "Appliquer les paramètres", "Commandes en cours",
  "Objets autorisés". ALL-CAPS keys stay ALL-CAPS, with accents kept (ÉCRAN, BÊTA, TERMINÉ).
- **"(Hold) X" prompts:** "(Maintenir) X" with X in the infinitive — "(Maintenir) Consommer",
  "(Maintenir) Fumer", "(Maintenir) Sniffer".
- **Mouse / keys:** "Click" → "Cliquez (sur)", "Click and hold" → "Maintenez le clic (sur)",
  "Press <Input_X>" → "Appuyez sur <Input_X>", "Hold <Input_X>" → "Maintenez <Input_X>",
  right/left mouse button → "clic droit / clic gauche". SPACEBAR → ESPACE.
- **Typography (keep it simple):** a regular space (no non-breaking space) before `: ? ! ;` —
  "Quantité :", "Êtes-vous sûr ?", "GROS GAIN !". Ellipsis stays three dots "..." (not "…").
  Quotes: « like this » with regular spaces inside. Apostrophe: straight `'`.
- **Money / units** stay as in the key: "$" in front ("${0}", "+${0} de bonus"), `g`, `mph`, `°F`, `oz`.
  Don't switch to "{0} $".
- **Proper names stay in Latin, untranslated:** people, brands, stores, places, strains, vehicles —
  Benzies, Gas-Mart, Bleuball's Boutique, Bud's Bar, Albert Hoover, Northtown, Downtown, OG Kush,
  SpeedGrow, PGR, Cuke, Viagor, Addy. Generic words around them are translated ("Graine d'OG Kush",
  "Influence des Benzies à Northville", "au Gas-Mart").
- **Gender:** ranks and employee titles use the masculine form (titles). For relationship/cartel status
  labels use the nouns below (gender-neutral). Face expressions agree with "visage" (masc.): "Agité", "Neutre".
- **Slang register (dialogue):** "herbe/beuh", "matos", "fric/thune", "flics", "keuf" are fine in NPC
  speech; UI text stays neutral (argent, police, produit).

## Additions from chunks 01–10

Conventions:
- **Percent:** space before % — "{0} %", "100 % naturel".
- **Times in UI:** sentences use "{0}h du matin / du soir" ("Réveil à {0}h du matin", curfew boards
  "{0}H DU SOIR - {1}H DU MATIN"); compact clock/range widgets keep the key's AM/PM ("{0} AM", "{0}AM-{1}PM").
  Durations: "{0} h {1} min", "{0} min", "{0} s".
- **Numbering:** "#2" → "n°2" ("Chambre de motel n°2", "Mot amical n°1").
- **Tokens that are NOT placeholders stay untouched:** `<Employee>` (the validator counts it as a tag),
  `formal_address`, internal ids (`ExpiringSoon`, `FadedText`, `CentralCanalDropItemCount`, `FF7A7A`).
- **Location descriptions** (lowercase keys, inserted via `<LOCATION>`): lowercase prepositional phrases —
  "derrière la banque", "au skatepark", "sous le pont ouest", "devant le motel"
  ("Je serai <LOCATION>", "on se retrouve <LOCATION> entre <WINDOW_START> et <WINDOW_END>").
  Capitalized location names: "Derrière la banque", "À côté du tribunal".
- **<PRODUCT> carries its own quantity** ("2x OG Kush"): no article before it — "T'as <PRODUCT> ?",
  "Je peux avoir <PRODUCT> ?", "Je te le paie <PRICE>".
- **Acronym keys stay acronyms:** FOV, SFX, TV, SSAO, PGR, RDX (RV → Camping-car, see above).
- Albert Hoover tutoie but says "chef" for "sir".
