# Spanish glossary (es)

Neutral Spanish, understandable in Spain and Latin America. Consult this before translating a chunk
and follow it strictly; add new recurring terms as you go (never change an existing entry silently).

## Conventions

- **Register**: the game addresses the player with **tú** everywhere — UI instructions, tooltips,
  system messages ("Haz clic", "¿Estás seguro?", "tu informe"). Dialogue: NPCs use **tú** and slang;
  formal NPCs (police officers, bank/shop clerks, the realtor) may use **usted**.
- **Buttons / actions / input prompts**: infinitive — "Comprar", "Cerrar", "Aceptar", "Aplicar ajustes",
  "Añadir al carrito". Instructions and hints: imperative tú — "Elige", "Acepta la oferta", "Haz clic".
- **"(Hold) X"** → **"(Mantener) X"** with X in infinitive: "(Mantener) Consumir", "(Mantener) Fumar".
- **Click / Press / Hold**: "Click" → "Haz clic"; "Click and hold" → "Mantén presionado/a";
  "Press" → "Presiona"; "Right mouse button" → "botón derecho del ratón". Use *presionar*, never *pulsar/apretar*.
- **Neutral vocabulary**: avoid regionalisms. Never use *coger* (use *tomar/recoger/agarrar*), *vale*,
  *ordenador*, *coche* (use *vehículo/auto* — "car" → "auto"), *pisos* (use *apartamentos*), *guay/chévere*.
  Prefer: *añadir* (not *agregar*), *hongos* (not *setas*), *efectivo* (cash), *celular* is NOT used — "phone" → *teléfono*.
- **Punctuation**: always open ¿ and ¡ ("¿Estás seguro?", "¡GRAN PREMIO!", "¡BLACKJACK!"). Keep "..." as three
  dots (not "…"). Keys ending with `:` / `...` / `?` keep that ending. ALL-CAPS keys → ALL-CAPS (with accents: "MÁS").
- **Capitalization**: sentence case for labels and titles ("Clientes asignados", "Operaciones de blanqueo"),
  not English Title Case. Ranks, effects and quality levels are capitalized as labels ("Barón III", "Calmante").
- **Proper names stay in Latin spelling**: people, brands, stores, places, strains, products, vehicles
  (Benzies, Gas-Mart, Northtown, Downtown, Bleuball's Boutique, Bud's Bar, Albert Hoover, OG Kush, SpeedGrow, PGR,
  Bungalow). Plural of the cartel: "los Benzies"; "Benzies' influence" → "influencia de los Benzies".
- **Money / units**: "$" stays in front, no space: "${0}", "+${0} de bonificación". Units unchanged (g, mph, °F, oz).
- **Gender of status adjectives**: relationship and cartel statuses are masculine singular (agree with
  *el contacto / el cártel*); vote/poll states are feminine (agree with *la votación*: "ACTIVA", "CERRADA");
  character-creator face expressions are masculine (agree with *el gesto*: "Agitado").
- **Game title** SCHEDULE I is never translated.
