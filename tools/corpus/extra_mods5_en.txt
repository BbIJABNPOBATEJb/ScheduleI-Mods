Buyers
Buyers color
Your customers who would buy from you right now: no deal arranged or offered, not served by one of your dealers, and no purchase in the last few hours.
People you can win over with a free sample (friends of your customers), except those who already turned you down today.
Outline targets
The people and stashes the arrows point at glow in the arrow's color, even through walls.
Others' hits within
Hits by other players or between characters show a number and a bar only this close to you, and only if nothing blocks your view. Your own hits always show.
